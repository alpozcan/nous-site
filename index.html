One edition of AI news a day.

Nous AI Newsletter reads the public sources so you do not have to: roughly twelve to twenty ranked items each day, each with a short written summary, plus an executive brief of the day.

iOS · coming to the App Store

What it does

  • A fixed daily edition

    An edition is frozen when it is published, so it will not shuffle under you during the day. Every item links to the original, and the original is the authority.

  • Ranked, not endless

    Twelve to twenty items, selected and ordered by software rather than by a feed that never ends. Quiet days are genuinely quieter.

  • Public sources only

    RSS and Atom feeds from news outlets, research labs and companies; Hacker News; arXiv; and public posts from a curated list of accounts on X.

  • An executive brief

    A short written summary of the day, so you can read the shape of it in a minute and go deeper only where it matters.

  • Archive and search

    Nous+ adds every past edition, search across the archive, muting of topics and sources, and the daily notification.

  • No sign-in, ever

    There is no account. The app proves to the server that it is a genuine copy of itself using Apple App Attest. Nothing to remember, nothing to leak.

Privacy

No accounts. No analytics. No advertising. No third-party trackers.

Nous never asks who you are, and the server has no way of finding out. It keeps one anonymous identifier per installed copy of the app, and — only if you switch notifications on — the settings a notification needs. Nothing about you is sold or shared for advertising, and no IP address is ever written to the database.

Everything the server keeps fits in two tables, and the privacy policy lists both of them, column by column.
