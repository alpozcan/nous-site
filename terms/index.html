What Nous+ costs, what it includes, how the free trial and renewal work, and how to cancel.

There is no separate custom licence. The terms on this page are the commercial terms of the subscription, which sit alongside it.

What Nous is

Nous is a daily edition of ranked artificial-intelligence news. Each day it reads publicly available sources — RSS and Atom feeds, Hacker News, arXiv, and public posts from a curated list of accounts on X — ranks what it finds, and publishes a fixed edition of roughly twelve to twenty items with automatically written summaries and a short executive brief.

The subscription

  • What it is called: Nous+.
  • Length of a period: one month, renewing automatically.
  • Price: US$1.99 per month in the United States storefront. Apple converts and displays the local price in your own storefront, and the exact amount is shown in the app before you confirm anything.
  • Free trial: 1 week, once per Apple ID. Nothing is charged during it.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at the end of the free trial, and then at the start of each renewal period. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Cancelling

Cancel at any time — during the trial or after — at https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions, or in Settings on your device under your Apple ID, then Subscriptions. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the subscription keeps working until the end of the period you have already paid for. Cancelling during the free trial stops it before any charge is made.

Any unused part of a free trial is forfeited if you buy the subscription before the trial ends. Refunds are handled by Apple, not by Alp Özcan: request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

What the summaries are, and are not

Nous does not write the news. It selects from what other people published and summarises it automatically, using large language models. Summaries can be incomplete, out of date, or simply wrong, and ranking is a judgement made by software. Every item links to the original, and the original is the authority.

Nothing in Nous is financial, legal, investment or professional advice, and none of it should be relied on as the sole basis for a decision.

Third-party content

Headlines, excerpts and links belong to the publications that produced them, and are shown here under the terms on which those publications make their feeds available. Nous does not reproduce full articles. If you are a publisher and want your feed removed, or you believe something here infringes your rights, write to hi@alp.me and it will be dealt with promptly.

Public posts from X are shown with their author and a link to the original. They are third-party statements, not statements by Nous. If a post — or any other item — shown in an edition is objectionable, report it from the item in the app, or by writing to hi@alp.me. Reports are read by a person, and an item that should not be there is removed from the service.

Using the service

The app's connection to the server is authenticated, and the service is for use through the app. Do not attempt to access it by other means, to bulk-extract the archive, or to redistribute the editions as your own product. Permission to use the service may be withdrawn for abuse.

Availability and changes

Nous is offered as it is, without a guarantee that it will be available at any particular time, that any particular source will keep being carried, or that an edition will be published on any given day. Sources, ranking and features can change. If the service is discontinued, active subscriptions will be allowed to lapse rather than being renewed, and Apple's refund process remains available.

If these terms change, the date at the top of this page changes with them and this page is the current version.
